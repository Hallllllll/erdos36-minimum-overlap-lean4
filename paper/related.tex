\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Erd{\H{o}}s posed the minimum overlap problem in 1955. It is Problem~36
in Bloom's catalogue~\cite{Erdos1955,BloomErdos36}. Haugland used
Swinnerton-Dyer's characterization to obtain an upper bound of
$0.382002\ldots$ with 21 cells in 1996 and later reported
$0.3809268534330870$ with 51 cells~\cite{Haugland1996,Haugland2016}.
White proved $\ct>0.379005$ using Fourier analysis and convex
optimization~\cite{White2023}.

AlphaEvolve reported $\ct\le0.380924$ via evolutionary
search~\cite[Appendix~B.5]{Novikov2025AlphaEvolve}.
EinsteinArena made the objective a shared optimization task with a public
Python verifier~\cite[\S2.3 and Appendix~A.2]{Bianchi2026EinsteinArena}.
Our witness refines the 4,704-cell Compute Substrate proposal of
25 November 2026, which credits EinsteinArena's Blob lineage.
The archived heights give a parent supremum of
$\tfrac{123113681346619}{323255185441944}$ and a gain of more than
$1.14531579911\times10^{-9}$ for our refinement~\cite{ComputeSubstrate2026Parent,ComputeSubstrate2026ParentData}.

On 27 November 2026, Patrick White and Claude reported in
\emph{Erd{\H{o}}s Problem a Day} that two separately written exact
computations confirmed $U$ and its unique maximizing shift for the
v1.0.0 witness~\cite{ErdosProblemADay2026Review}. The review inspected
the v1.0.0 Lean source and relied on project CI for build evidence,
including for \texttt{31ded31}. On the same day, Kirschenman's 18,816-cell
witness in open PR~\#419 superseded $U$ with
\[
  \frac{270730612195671389820648367}{710848381933401954727231488}
  < 0.3808556355,
\]
which the review also recomputed exactly~\cite{Kirschenman2026PR419,ErdosProblemADay2026Review}.

Computational reflection underlies Gonthier's reducibility checks in Coq
and Chaieb and Nipkow's quantifier elimination in
Isabelle/HOL~\cite{Gonthier2008,ChaiebNipkow2008}. In Lean~4,
\texttt{decide +kernel} reduces the numerical predicates in the
kernel~\cite{deMouraUllrich2021,Lean436Decide}. Our generic certificate
uses single-evaluation Kronecker substitution~\cite[\S1]{Harvey2009}.

The discrete limit proof links exact-mass cell averaging to
simultaneous rounding. The endpoint selection resembles the
method of conditional expectations used to derandomize rounding
schemes~\cite{Raghavan1988}. Here coordinate-wise affinity
eliminates the first-order term in a quartic potential, and a
separate cardinality repair uses the bounded incidence of each
site at a given lag.

Xu's preliminary open-source Lean development proves convergence
of the normalized discrete minima to their liminf by a Fekete
argument and verifies several classical bounds
and finite values~\cite{Xu2026MinimumOverlap}. The convergence
theorem here identifies the discrete limit with the infimum over
admissible real densities by establishing the comparison in both
directions.

The bundled \texttt{leanchecker} tool replays compiled declarations
through Lean's kernel~\cite{MorrisonUllrichLeanchecker436}.
